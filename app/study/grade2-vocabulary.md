# Grade 2 — Word Detective (Vocabulary)

You meet a word you've never seen. Don't panic — a word detective has
**five tools**:

## Tool 1 — Context clues

Let the OTHER words in the sentence tell you:

> "The **enormous** elephant was *too big to fit through the door*."

Too big to fit? *Enormous* must mean **very big**. The clue was right there.

## Tool 2 — Prefix clues

> **un**kind = NOT kind · **re**build = build AGAIN · **pre**game = BEFORE
> the game

## Tool 3 — Root words

Find the little word hiding inside: know **help**, and you can unlock
help**er**, help**ful**, and help**less**.

## Tool 4 — Compound words

Read the two words inside: a **birdhouse** is a house for birds; a
**bookshelf** is a shelf for books; a **raincoat** keeps rain off your coat.
⚠️ But watch out — a **butterfly** is NOT a fly made of butter! Some
compound words keep their meaning secret.

## Tool 5 — Look it up

The **glossary** (back of the book) or a **dictionary** lists words A to Z
and tells you what they mean.

## Words with two jobs

Some words have more than one meaning — the **sentence** tells you which:

- "A **bat** flew out of the cave" (animal) vs. "swing the **bat**" (baseball)
- "the dog's **bark**" (sound) vs. "the tree's **bark**" (covering)
- "an elephant's **trunk**" vs. "a tree **trunk**" vs. "the car's **trunk**"

## Sound-alike words (homophones)

- **their** = belongs to them · **there** = a place · **they're** = they are
- **to** = toward · **too** = also · **two** = 2
- **its** = belongs to it · **it's** = it is
- know/no · write/right · hear/here (**hear** has an **ear** in it!)

## Shades of meaning

Some words are almost twins, but one is **stronger**:

> toss → throw → **hurl**
> glance → look → stare → **glare**
> whisper → say → shout → **scream**
> glad → happy → thrilled → **overjoyed**
> annoyed → mad → angry → **furious**
> big → large → huge → **enormous**

- **Synonyms** = near-twins: big/large, fast/quick, happy/glad
- **Antonyms** = opposites: hot/cold, empty/full, day/night

Picking the exact right shade makes your writing come alive: a *gentle
toss*, an *angry hurl*.
