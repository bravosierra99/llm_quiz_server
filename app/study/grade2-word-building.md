# Grade 2 — Word Building (Prefixes, Suffixes & Endings)

Words are built like Lego. There's a **root word** in the middle — the
smallest piece that means something on its own — and you can snap pieces on:

> un + **help** + ful &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; re + **read** + ing

If you know the root and what each piece means, you can figure out words
you've **never even seen before**.

## Prefixes — pieces that snap on the FRONT

| Prefix | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **un-** | not, opposite of | **un**happy = not happy · **un**lock, **un**fair |
| **re-** | again | **re**do = do again · **re**tell, **re**read |
| **pre-** | before | **pre**heat = heat before · **pre**school, **pre**view |
| **dis-** | not, opposite of | **dis**agree = not agree · **dis**like, **dis**appear |
| **mis-** | wrongly | **mis**spell = spell wrong · **mis**place, **mis**behave |

## Suffixes — pieces that snap on the END

| Suffix | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **-ful** | full of | joy**ful** = full of joy · helpful, careful |
| **-less** | without | fear**less** = without fear · careless, helpless |
| **-ly** | in a ___ way | quick**ly** = in a quick way · slowly, kindly |
| **-er** | more (compares 2) | tall**er** — "Sam is taller than Max" |
| **-est** | most (compares 3+) | tall**est** — "Sam is the tallest in class" |
| **-er** | a person who ___s | bak**er** = a person who bakes · teacher, farmer |
| **-ness** | being ___ | kind**ness** = being kind · darkness, sadness |
| **-y** | having, like | rain**y**, sunn**y**, sleep**y**, luck**y** |

## The -ed ending has THREE sounds

-ed always means "it already happened," but listen:

- sounds like **/t/**: jump**ed**, walk**ed**, laugh**ed**
- sounds like **/d/**: play**ed**, smil**ed**, call**ed**
- sounds like **/id/** (a whole extra beat): want**ed**, need**ed** — only
  after t or d

## Spelling rules for adding endings

Four rules cover almost everything:

1. **Doubling rule** — short word, one vowel, one final consonant?
   **Double it**: run → ru**nn**ing, hop → ho**pp**ed, big → bi**gg**est
2. **Drop the e** — silent e? Drop it first: bake → bak**ing**,
   smile → smil**ed**, nice → nic**er**
3. **Y rule** — consonant + y? Change y to **i**: cry → cr**i**ed,
   happy → happ**i**er... **but keep the y before -ing**: cry → cr**y**ing
4. **-es rule** — after s, x, z, ch, sh you need **-es**: bus → bus**es**,
   fox → fox**es**, wish → wish**es**. And consonant + y: baby → bab**ies**

**Spot-it trick:** *hopped* has two p's because "hop" had a short o to
protect. *Hoped* has one p because it came from "hope" — the silent e was
already there.
