# Grade 2 — Reading Big Words (Syllables)

A big word is just **small words holding hands**. Each chunk is called a
**syllable**, and every syllable has exactly **one vowel sound**. Clap as you
say a word and you'll count them: rab-bit (2 claps), but-ter-fly (3 claps).

If you can chop a big word into syllables, you can read **any** word — each
chunk follows the rules you already know.

## How to chop

**Two consonants between vowels? Split between them.**

> rab**b**it → rab / bit &nbsp;&nbsp; win**t**er → win / ter &nbsp;&nbsp; bas**k**et → bas / ket

The first chunk ends in a consonant (closed), so its vowel is **short**.

**One consonant between vowels? Split BEFORE it.**

> tiger → ti / ger &nbsp;&nbsp; paper → pa / per &nbsp;&nbsp; music → mu / sic

Now the first chunk ends with its vowel (open), so the vowel is **long** —
that's why tiger is "TIE-ger", not "TIG-er"!

*If that makes a silly word, split after instead:* cab / in, riv / er,
lem / on (first vowel short).

**Compound word? Split between the two small words.**

> sun / set &nbsp;&nbsp; cup / cake &nbsp;&nbsp; base / ball

**Ends in -le? Count back three letters.**

> ta / ble &nbsp;&nbsp; lit / tle &nbsp;&nbsp; pur / ple

## The six kinds of syllables

| Type | Clue | Vowel sound | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Closed** | ends in a consonant | short | rab, nap, in |
| **Open** | ends with the vowel | long | ti, mu, ba, go |
| **Silent e** | vowel-consonant-e | long | cake (cup-cake) |
| **Vowel team** | two vowels together | the team's sound | rain (rain-bow) |
| **Bossy r** | vowel + r | the r sound | gar (gar-den) |
| **-le** | consonant + le | "ul" | ble (ta-ble) |

## Try it

- **robot** → ro / bot — "ro" is open, so the first o is long: "ROW-bot"
- **napkin** → nap / kin — both closed, both short
- **cupcake** → cup / cake — closed + silent e
- **turtle** → tur / tle — bossy r + le

Once you see the chunks, big words stop being scary — they're just two or
three little words standing in a row.
