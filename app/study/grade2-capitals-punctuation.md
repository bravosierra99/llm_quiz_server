# Grade 2 — Capitals, Commas & Apostrophes

## What gets a capital letter?

1. The **first word** of every sentence
2. The word **I** — always
3. **Names** of people and pets: Jessica, Dr. Lee, Rex
4. **Holidays**: Thanksgiving, Halloween, the Fourth of July
5. **Brand names**: Lego, Cheerios — but the plain thing stays small:
   *blocks*, *cereal*
6. **Place names**: Annapolis, Maryland, the Chesapeake Bay, the Atlantic
   Ocean — but plain words like *city* and *river* stay small
7. **Days and months**: Monday, July

> ✏️ "we visited annapolis on thanksgiving" needs THREE fixes:
> "**W**e visited **A**nnapolis on **T**hanksgiving."

## Commas in a friendly letter

A friendly letter has five parts — **date, greeting, body, closing,
signature** — and the greeting and closing each end with a **comma**:

> Dear Grandma**,**
>
> Thank you for the cookies. They were the best ones ever!
>
> Love**,**
> Jessica

## Apostrophes job #1 — contractions

A **contraction** squeezes two words into one. The apostrophe marks the spot
where letters **fell out**:

| Contraction | Long way | Contraction | Long way |
|---|---|---|---|
| isn't | is not | I'm | I am |
| don't | do not | you're | you are |
| can't | cannot | they're | they are |
| didn't | did not | we'll | we will |
| **won't** | will not (the weird one!) | let's | let us |

## Apostrophes job #2 — showing who owns it

Add **'s** to show something belongs to someone:

> the **dog's** bone · **Maria's** book · the **teacher's** desk

## The its / it's trap

- **it's** = **it is**: "It's raining."
- **its** (no apostrophe) = **belongs to it**: "The bird flapped its wings."

**Spot-it trick:** try saying "it is" in the sentence. "The bird flapped
*it is* wings"? Nope — so no apostrophe!
