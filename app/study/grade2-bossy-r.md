# Grade 2 — Bossy R & Tricky Spellings

## Bossy R

The letter **r** is bossy. When it stands right after a vowel, it **takes
over** — the vowel isn't long OR short anymore. It makes a brand-new sound:

| Spelling | Sound | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **ar** | /ar/ | car, farm, star, shark |
| **or** | /or/ | corn, fork, storm, horse |
| **er** | /er/ | her, fern, sister |
| **ir** | /er/ | bird, girl, first, shirt |
| **ur** | /er/ | fur, turn, purple, Thursday |

**The big secret:** er, ir, and ur all make **exactly the same sound**!
H**er**, b**ir**d, and f**ur** rhyme in the middle. That means your ears
can't tell you which spelling to use — you have to **remember** which word
uses which. (Her sister... the first bird... purple fur on Thursday.)

A few more r-teams: **ore** (more, store), and **are** / **air** which both
say "air" — c**are**, sh**are**, h**air**, ch**air**.

## Soft and hard c and g

**c** and **g** each have two sounds, and the NEXT letter decides:

- Before **e, i, or y** → **soft**: **c** says /s/ (city, cent, pencil) and
  **g** says /j/ (gem, giant, gym, cage)
- Before anything else → **hard**: cat, cup, clap — gate, go, gum

**Exceptions to memorize:** get, give, girl, gift, begin — hard g even
though e or i comes next.

## Silent letters

Some letters are just... quiet. You write them but never say them:

| Pattern | Silent letter | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **kn** | k | knee, know, knock |
| **wr** | w | write, wrong, wrap |
| **mb** | b | lamb, comb, thumb, climb |

## End-of-word patterns: the "short vowel gets the big spelling" rule

Right after a **short vowel**, these ending sounds use their **bigger**
spelling:

| Sound | After a short vowel | After anything else |
|---|---|---|
| /k/ | **-ck** — duck, stick, clock | -k or -ke — book, bake |
| /ch/ | **-tch** — catch, match, pitch | -ch — bench, teach |
| /j/ | **-dge** — badge, edge, judge | -ge — cage, huge, large |

And the **FLOSS rule**: after a short vowel, a final **f, l, s, or z**
doubles — stu**ff**, be**ll**, mi**ss**, bu**zz**.

**Spot-it trick:** hear a short vowel? The ending spelling gets **extra
letters** to protect it — *badge* needs the d, *cage* doesn't.
