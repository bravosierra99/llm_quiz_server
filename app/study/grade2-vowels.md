# Grade 2 — Long Vowels, Silent E & Vowel Teams

Every vowel — **a, e, i, o, u** — knows two sounds:

- its **short** sound: **cat, bed, sit, hot, cup**
- its **long** sound, where it **says its own name**: c**a**ke, h**e**, b**i**ke, b**o**ne, c**u**te

The trick is knowing **which sound to use**. Good news: there are rules!

## Rule 1 — Closed syllable = short vowel

If the vowel is **closed in** by a consonant at the end, it's usually **short**:

> c**a**p, h**i**d, h**o**p, c**u**b

## Rule 2 — Open syllable = long vowel

If the word **ends with the vowel**, the vowel is out in the open and says
its **name**:

> h**e**, g**o**, h**i**, m**y**

## Rule 3 — Silent E, the magic letter

A final **e** is **silent** — you never say it. Its whole job is to reach back
and make the earlier vowel **say its name**:

| Short word | + e | New word |
|---|---|---|
| cap | → | c**a**pe |
| kit | → | k**i**te |
| hop | → | h**o**pe |
| cub | → | c**u**be |
| plan | → | pl**a**ne |

The magic e changes the **meaning** too — a *kit* is a set of things, but a
*kite* flies in the sky!

## Rule 4 — Vowel teams

Two vowels can **team up** to make one sound. Often, *"when two vowels go
walking, the first one does the talking"* — the first vowel says its name:

| Team | Sound | Examples | Where it likes to be |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ai** | long a | rain, mail, train | middle of a word |
| **ay** | long a | play, day, stay | end of a word |
| **ee** | long e | see, feet, tree | anywhere |
| **ea** | long e | eat, team, beach | (but watch out below!) |
| **oa** | long o | boat, coat, road | middle |
| **ow** | long o | snow, grow, show | end |
| **igh** | long i | light, night, right | before t |
| **oo** | "moon" | food, zoo, school | — |
| **ou** | "ow!" | out, house, cloud | middle |
| **oi / oy** | "oy!" | coin (middle), boy (end) | — |
| **au / aw** | "aw" | because (middle), saw (end) | — |

**Spot-it trick for spelling:** teams like **ai, oa, oi, au** go in the
**middle** of words; their partners **ay, ow, oy, aw** go at the **end**.

## Sneaky teams with TWO sounds

Some teams can't make up their minds. If the first sound makes a silly word,
**try the other one**:

- **ea** — long e in *team*, but short e in *bread* and *head*
- **oo** — like *moon* (food, zoo) or like *book* (good, foot)
- **ow** — long o in *snow*, but "ow!" in *cow* and *town*
- **ie** — long i in *pie*, but long e in *field*

You already know more of these words than you think — the rules just explain
what your eyes have been seeing all along.
